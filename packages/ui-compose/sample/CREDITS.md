# Sample credits

The photos used by the Liquid Glass playground live in `src/main/res/drawable-nodpi` of this sample only; they are not part of
the `ui-compose` kit artifact. Each was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, resized to 1280 px wide and re-encoded as WebP
(quality 72, under 200 KB). All three are published under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), so no attribution is
required; it is given anyway. The credits are also shown in the playground.
