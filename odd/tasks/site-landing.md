# Site landing and Liquid Glass playground (feat/site-landing)

## Objective
Owner request 2026-10-12: "leave a complete landing of all we offer" plus a standalone Liquid Glass playground (slider panel with every `LiquidGlassOptions` field, real photo backdrop, freely draggable glass panel). UX idea only from ybouane's playground; no code or assets copied. Priority addendum: a "crystal" look (thick clear convex lens that minifies the backdrop, rim aberration, no frost, no veil) that is the playground's default.
